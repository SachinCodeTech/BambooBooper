Panda Arcade Adventure · v1.0

🎮 The Game

Guide a hungry panda through a bamboo forest in this classic snake-style arcade game. Eat bamboo to grow longer, level up through 6 stages, and unlock special panda skins — all while avoiding walls and your own tail!

✨ Features

6 Skins 6 Levels Speed Control Score Cards Offline Play Sound FX Haptics PWA

🕹 How to Play

  • Use the D-pad or swipe on the canvas to move your panda
  • Eat 🎋 bamboo to score and grow your tail
  • Avoid hitting walls or your own body
  • Reach Level 6 to unlock all 6 panda skins
  • Use Speed control to ramp up the challenge

🔒 Privacy Policy

🔒 Our Commitment

CodeTech is committed to your privacy. This policy explains exactly what Bamboo Booper does — and does not do — with your data.

Effective: April 01, 2026

🚫 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Name, email, or personal identifiers
  • Location or GPS data
  • Device or advertising IDs
  • Analytics or usage telemetry
  • Payment or financial data
  • Camera, microphone, or contacts

💾 Local Storage Only

The following data is stored only on your device, never transmitted:

  • Your player name (for score screen)
  • Your high score
  • Selected panda skin
  • App settings (sound, haptics, etc.)

🌐 Third-Party CDN

The jsPDF library is loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com for PDF score cards. This is a standard CDN request — no personal data is transmitted. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details.

👶 Children's Privacy

Bamboo Booper is family-friendly and suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
